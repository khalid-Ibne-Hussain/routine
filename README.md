# 🎯 Master Daily & Weekend Routine

> **Goal:** Balance Job Output (R Programming) + Career Growth (Data Engineering) + English Skills.

---

## 📅 Working Days (Saturday – Wednesday)

```
08:00 AM – 06:00 PM | Office Time
  ├── Slot 1 (1 Hr) : 💻 R Programming (Job Priority: dplyr, tidyverse, data cleanup)
  └── Slot 2 (1 Hr) : 🌐 English & Tech Reading (Medium / Towards Data Science articles)

06:00 PM – 08:00 PM | Commute, Rest & Dinner

08:30 PM – 09:30 PM | 🛠️ Data Engineering Core (1 Hr: SQL / Python / Database Concepts)
09:30 PM – 10:15 PM | 🎧 English Listening & Podcast (45 Mins: Tech/Self-improvement)
10:15 PM – 10:30 PM | 📝 Tomorrow's Prep & Checklist Review (15 Mins)

11:00 PM            | 😴 Sleep (Mandatory for 7 AM wakeup)
```

> **Flexibility Rule (If returning home late):**  
> Skip the podcast, do **45 Mins Data Engineering**, then prep for tomorrow and sleep by 11:00 PM.

---

## 📅 Weekend / Off-Days (Thursday & Friday)

### 📌 Thursday (Deep Focus Day)
```
07:00 AM – 08:30 AM | 🛠️ Data Engineering (1.5 Hrs: Complex SQL / Pipelines)
08:30 AM – 09:30 AM | 🍳 Breakfast & Break
09:30 AM – 11:00 AM | 💻 R Programming Project / Weekly Practice (1.5 Hrs)
11:00 AM – 05:00 PM | 🌿 Lunch, Rest & Personal Time
05:00 PM – 06:30 PM | 🗣️ English & Tech Article Summary (1.5 Hrs)
08:00 PM onwards    | 🌙 Personal Time & Sleep by 11:00 PM
```

### 📌 Friday (Light Review & Total Rest Day)
```
07:00 AM – 09:00 AM | 🔍 Weekly Review & Documentation (2 Hrs: Summarize R & DE progress)
09:00 AM onwards    | 🎉 100% FREE TIME (Family, rest, no studies!)
```

---

## ⚡ 3 Golden Rules to Beat Procrastination

1. **2-Minute Rule:** Feel lazy? Tell yourself to code or read for just 2 minutes.
2. **Pomodoro:** Work for 25 mins ➔ Take a 5 min break.
3. **Night Prep:** Open tomorrow's topics/tabs on your PC before going to bed.
